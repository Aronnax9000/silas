# Hamster from Montgomery 

In an Alabama classroom  
I was born with some misgivings  
in a cozy nest of shavings  
from an old Georgia pine  
Mama had post partum,  
she ate all my siblings  
a seventh grader took me home  
said you'll be just fine  

Make me a hamster  
that flies from Montgomery  
make me an entrant  
in the state rodent show  
just give me some hardwood  
that I can gnaw onto  
cos running in this wheel  
seems such a long way to go  

One day they placed me  
in a clear ball of plastic  
the puppy came and chased me  
and I rolled down the stairs  
in a frisson of excitement  
I bounced off the landing  
flew down the next flight  
and caught me some air  

I am an old hamster  
I sleep in on Sundays
our tails are short and stubby 
but our life stories long  



With our cheeks stuffed with millet  
won't you all sing along
